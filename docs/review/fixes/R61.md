**What changed.** `src/submeter/core/models.py` gains a frozen `GroupTotals` record after `Finding`: `cpu_s`, `io_read_bytes` and `io_write_bytes` as `int | None`, and `processes`. A workload in it reports the group's CPU, process count and, when the group can read it, I/O, labelled `kernel`, even when polling saw none of its processes.
