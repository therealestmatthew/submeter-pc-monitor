## Uses

`/sys/fs/cgroup/**/cpu.stat`, `/proc/PID/smaps_rollup`, `time.process_time()`.

## Provides

- `linux_cgroup_cost.py`: lists leaf cgroups, reads each `cpu.stat` for 100 rounds, and prints the leaf count, the median and worst CPU per round, the cost per leaf, and the share of one core at 2 s and at 5 s. Read for P0.6 and B5.
- `linux_pss_cost.py`: reads `smaps_rollup` for every readable pid once and prints the process count, resident GB, CPU ms for the round and ms per GB resident; and, under CQ-07's default, the resident GB of the 25 largest processes and the CPU ms of one round over only those 25. Read for P0.7 and B6.
- A step in the Linux job of `ci.yml` that runs both and keeps their output as an artefact (CQ-06's default: artefact `linux-costs`, files `linux-cgroup-cost.txt` and `linux-pss-cost.txt`, the names gate P0 saves them under in `docs/review/ground-windows/ci/`).

## Work

1. The two scripts run like `scripts/platform_check.py`. Parsing and statistics are functions the tests call; reading `/sys` and `/proc` happens only in `main()`. On a system without `/sys/fs/cgroup` or `/proc`, `main()` says so and exits non-zero. Each output's first line is `CI-runner figure` followed by what the figure is.
2. A pid that disappears or refuses a read during the round is skipped and counted; the count is printed.
3. The `ci.yml` step: Linux job only, after pytest, with `if: ${{ !cancelled() && steps.sync.outcome == 'success' }}` like every step after `sync` (P0-W1-T1's test checks this), writing both outputs into a folder under the runner's temporary folder (outside the working copy, so the last `git status --porcelain` step stays empty) and uploading it as the artefact. A script that exits non-zero fails the job, like any other step.
4. The thresholds are the owner's (B5, B6). The scripts print figures; they decide nothing.
