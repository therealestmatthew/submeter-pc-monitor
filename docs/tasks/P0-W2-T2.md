## Goal

A probe the owner runs in three shells answers, with recorded numbers, the questions the tagged-run design (B1) depends on: can a standard user create a Job Object and a child inside a new one, do a job's CPU totals match its children's, can a job be reopened by name while a holder keeps a handle, what breakaway does, how job CPU is quantised, and whether a parent job's totals include a nested job's processes.

## Work

Steps a to h of gate P0.8, each printing its result:

- a. Create a job as the user.
- b. Create a child inside a new job (`PROC_THREAD_ATTRIBUTE_JOB_LIST`) while the probe may itself be in a job; record `IsProcessInJob(GetCurrentProcess(), NULL)`.
- c. After every child has exited, the job's CPU totals against each child's `GetProcessTimes`, read on handles held until the end; pass is within 2%, with ActiveProcesses 0.
- d. Reopen the job by name after its creator exits while a holder keeps a handle (expected to succeed), then after the holder closes it (expected to fail with error 2).
- e. A direct child with `CREATE_BREAKAWAY_FROM_JOB` in a job without `JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_BREAKAWAY_OK`: record the error code.
- f. With `JOB_OBJECT_LIMIT_BREAKAWAY_OK` set, never `SILENT_BREAKAWAY_OK`: node, then bash, then a grandchild, and `IsProcessInJob(grandchild, job)`. Skipped with the reason when node or bash is absent (B16).
- g. 500 children busy 5 ms each: record the job CPU and the spacing of its values, to see whether it clusters at multiples of about 15.6 ms.
- h. A burner in a job nested inside a job the probe made: both jobs' CPU totals, to see whether the outer job's totals include the burner's within 2%, as R49's subtraction assumes (K14).

Then the decision, from the step results: steps d and h as expected: "the holder design stands"; step a or b failing: "stop and ask" (tagged runs are on the floor); step d or step h otherwise: "B1 goes to the owner".

Builder's choice, named in the report: the child program (for example `sys.executable -c` with a fixed busy loop), the job names, and how many children step c uses. Every child does fixed work and exits on its own; the probe never terminates a process. All Windows calls are made inside functions, so the module loads on Linux for the tests.
