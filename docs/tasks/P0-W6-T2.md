## Provides

`scripts/probes/bulk_snapshot.py`, run as `uv run python scripts/probes/bulk_snapshot.py`: prints, for each provider (the bulk prototype and psutil), interval (2 s, 5 s) and process count (the machine's own, and with 150 extra idle processes), the figures of gate P0.9: process count, CPU ms per tick, cost per process, share of one core; the probe's user and kernel CPU apart (`GetProcessTimes`); the raw store's share of the tick; private bytes as the largest of samples taken every 10 s; one USS round for the 25 largest processes, and the combined share of the tick and that round at the 5 s idle interval for a `uss_interval` of 30 s, 60 s and 5 minutes (K2); Defender's CPU time over the runs, or unavailable if a standard user cannot read it (K11); per-field availability for other users' processes; the 300-process figure derived from the two points; and the trigger result. The owner saves it as `docs/review/ground-windows/probes/bulk-snapshot.txt` in P0-O2. B2 decides P2-W2-T1 and P2-W5-T1.

## Work

1. A prototype bulk snapshot through `NtQuerySystemInformation(SystemProcessInformation)`, pid 0 excluded, parsed from structures of fixed-width ctypes types (`c_uint32`, `c_uint64`, `c_uint16` for UTF-16 text), whose sizes and field offsets the tests check against the documented 64-bit values.
2. Each provider drives the sampler, attribution and raw store, timing the whole tick: 10 minutes at a fixed 2 s and 10 minutes at a fixed 5 s per provider (40 minutes); then the same 40 minutes again with 150 extra idle processes the probe starts itself (80 minutes in all), so that the 300-process figure comes from two points. The extra processes do nothing and exit on their own when the probe signals them through a file or a pipe it owns; the probe never terminates a process. The raw file goes in a temporary folder and is deleted at the end.
3. The trigger (`docs/11-language.md:21-24`; P0.9): fires if the bulk provider at 300 processes uses over 60 ms of CPU per tick at 2 s (3% of one core), over 50 ms per tick at 5 s (1%), or over 83,886,080 private bytes. The psutil figures are reported, not judged (I56).
4. The durations and the number of extra processes are arguments with the plan's values as defaults, so the smoke test can run 3 short ticks.
